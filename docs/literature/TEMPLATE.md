# [N] Paper title

across all thirteen, so they can be compared later.

**Citation:** Authors, *Title*, Venue, Year. DOI or URL.

## 1. What problem does it attack?

A paragraph in your own words, not the abstract reworded.

## 2. What is the method?

The core idea in three or four sentences. If it is a front-end, what goes in
and what comes out. If it is a model, the shape.

## 3. What data?

Dataset and partition. If it is not ASVspoof 2019 LA, say so — the number
below is then not comparable with ours.

number there is worse than a blank.

## 5. What do we take from it?

What lands in our code, and in which phase or experiment. "Nothing" is a
perfectly common answer.

## 6. What do we not take, and why?

Usually out of scope, too expensive to train, or needs data we do not have.
